# Employee Onboarding Process

Owner: People Operations. This guide explains what new joiners can expect in their first months at Kestrel Systems.

## Before Your First Day

You will receive your laptop by courier at least three working days before your start date, together with instructions for activating your account. Your manager will assign you an onboarding buddy, a colleague from a neighbouring team who can answer day-to-day questions.

## Day One

- 09:00 Welcome session with People Operations, including identity checks and collection of your ID badge.
- 10:30 IT setup: sign-in, multi-factor authentication and the password manager.
- 12:00 Welcome lunch with your team.
- 14:00 Office tour and introductions to department leads.
- 16:00 First one-to-one meeting with your manager.

## Week One

Complete the mandatory Code of Conduct and Data Protection training modules in the Learning Portal within your first five working days. Your manager will agree your 30-60-90 day goals with you during your first week. Join the new-joiners channel to meet other people who started recently.

## First Month

By the end of your first month you should have completed all mandatory training, including security awareness training, met with each member of your immediate team, and shared your first weekly progress update with your manager.

## Probation

The standard probation period is six months. A mid-point review takes place at three months to discuss progress and any support you need. At the end of probation your manager confirms your appointment in writing.

Probation can be extended once by up to three months if more time is needed to meet the expectations of the role. During probation, either party can end employment with one week of notice.

## Equipment and Access

Standard equipment is a laptop, a monitor, a headset and a keyboard. Additional equipment can be requested through the Self Service portal. Access to systems beyond the standard set must be requested by your manager.
