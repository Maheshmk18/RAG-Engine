# Data Classification and Retention Policy

Owner: Legal and Security. This policy explains how information is classified, handled and kept.

## Classification Levels

Kestrel Systems uses four classification levels.

- Public: information approved for release, such as marketing material and published blog posts.
- Internal: general business information for employees, such as the intranet and team plans.
- Confidential: sensitive business information, such as contracts, financial forecasts and employee records.
- Restricted: the most sensitive information, including customer data, authentication secrets and security reports.

Every document owner is responsible for assigning a classification. If no classification is marked, treat the information as Confidential.

## Handling Rules

Confidential and Restricted information must only be stored in approved company systems. Restricted information must never be sent by email attachment; share it through the approved secure file-sharing service with access limited to named individuals. Restricted information must not be copied to personal devices or entered into external AI tools.

Printing Restricted information requires approval from the data owner.

## Retention Periods

- Financial and accounting records are kept for 7 years after the end of the financial year.
- Employee records are kept for 6 years after the employee leaves.
- Customer contract records are kept for 6 years after the contract ends.
- Email is retained for 3 years and then deleted automatically, unless it is under a legal hold.
- Internal chat messages are retained for 1 year.
- Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates are kept for 6 months.

## Legal Hold

When Legal places a legal hold on information, normal deletion is suspended until the hold is formally released, regardless of the retention periods above.

## Data Subject Requests

Requests from individuals to access or delete their personal data must be forwarded to privacy@kestrel.example immediately. Kestrel Systems responds to these requests within 30 days.

## Disposal

Information that has reached the end of its retention period must be securely deleted. Paper records are destroyed through the secure shredding service, and devices are wiped by the IT team before disposal.
