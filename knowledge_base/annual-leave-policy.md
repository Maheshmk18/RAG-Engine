# Annual Leave Policy

Owner: People Operations. Applies to all Kestrel Systems employees on a permanent or fixed-term contract.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in their country of employment. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement calculated from their contracted weekly hours. For example, an employee contracted for three days a week receives 15 days.

Employees who join or leave part-way through the year receive a proportional entitlement for the months they are employed. Leave accrues monthly at 2.08 days per month for full-time employees.

After five years of continuous service, the annual entitlement increases to 28 days. After ten years it increases to 30 days.

## Booking Leave

Leave requests are submitted through the HR Portal. Requests must be made at least two weeks before the first day of leave. Requests for more than five consecutive working days require one month of notice.

All requests are subject to manager approval, which is based on team coverage. Managers must respond to a request within five working days. A request that has not been approved is not confirmed, and employees should not book non-refundable travel until approval is given.

During the December holiday period, leave is allocated fairly across the team, and managers may ask employees to agree cover arrangements before approving.

## Carry-Over

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year, after which they are forfeited. Days above the carry-over limit are forfeited at the end of the year and are not paid out.

Exceptions to the carry-over limit are only granted where an employee could not take leave because of long-term sickness or parental leave. These exceptions require approval from People Operations.

## Sickness During Leave

If an employee becomes ill during annual leave, they can ask for the affected days to be reclassified as sick leave. A medical certificate covering the full period of illness is required, and the request must be made within seven days of returning to work. Reclassified days are returned to the employee's annual leave balance.

## Unpaid Leave

Employees may request up to 20 days of unpaid leave per year once their annual leave balance has been used. Unpaid leave requires approval from both the line manager and People Operations and does not accrue annual leave.

### Leave During Notice Period

Employees who resign may be asked to take outstanding leave during their notice period. Any accrued but untaken leave at the end of employment is paid out in the final salary payment. Leave taken in excess of the accrued amount is deducted from the final payment.
