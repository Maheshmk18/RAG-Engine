# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: Workplace Team. This policy sets out where and when Kestrel Systems employees work.

## Hybrid Working

Most roles at Kestrel Systems are hybrid. Hybrid employees work from the office at least two days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days, when teams are expected to be in the office together for planning and collaboration. The remaining days can be worked from home or from the office.

Teams can agree additional in-office days for onboarding, workshops or project milestones.

## Core Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Employees are expected to be available for meetings and messages during core hours. Outside core hours, employees can arrange their working time flexibly with their manager's agreement.

## Fully Remote Roles

A fully remote arrangement requires approval from the relevant Vice President and People Operations. Fully remote employees must attend in person for the quarterly company gathering and for their team's planning week.

## Working from Abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least four weeks in advance and need approval from the employee's manager and the Legal team, because working abroad can create tax and immigration obligations. Working from a country where Kestrel Systems is subject to sanctions restrictions is not permitted.

## Home Office Equipment and Allowance

Employees who work from home at least one day a week receive a one-time home office allowance of 500 for a desk, chair and other equipment. They also receive a monthly internet allowance of 30, paid through payroll. Company laptops and monitors remain the property of Kestrel Systems and must be returned when employment ends.

## Health and Safety at Home

Employees working from home must complete the home workstation self-assessment in the HR Portal within their first month. Work-related injuries at home should be reported to the Workplace Team within 24 hours.

## Security When Working Remotely

Remote work must follow the IT Security Guidelines, including the requirement to connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
