# Parental Leave Policy

Owner: People Operations. This policy covers leave for birth parents, partners, adoptive parents and surrogate parents.

## Primary Caregiver Leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to up to 26 weeks of parental leave. The first 16 weeks are paid at full salary, and the remaining 10 weeks are paid at 50 percent of salary. Primary caregiver leave can start up to 11 weeks before the expected week of childbirth.

Enhanced pay requires at least 12 months of continuous service at the start of the leave. Employees with less than 12 months of service receive statutory pay only, but keep the full 26-week leave entitlement.

## Secondary Caregiver Leave

The secondary caregiver, such as a partner, receives 4 weeks of leave at full pay. It can be taken as a single block or in two blocks of two weeks, within the first 12 months after the birth or placement. There is no minimum service requirement for secondary caregiver leave.

## Adoption and Surrogacy

Adoptive and surrogate parents receive the same entitlements as birth parents. Leave can start up to two weeks before the expected placement date. Employees can also take paid time off to attend up to five pre-adoption appointments.

## Giving Notice

Employees must tell People Operations about their intention to take parental leave at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth or placement. The planned start date can be changed with 28 days of notice.

## During Leave

Employees can work up to 10 keeping-in-touch days during parental leave without ending their leave. Keeping-in-touch days are paid at the normal daily rate and are optional for both the employee and the company.

Annual leave continues to accrue during parental leave. Health insurance and pension contributions continue at the normal employer rate for the full period of leave.

## Returning to Work

Employees returning from parental leave can return to the same role. Employees may request flexible working when they return, and requests will be considered in line with the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy. A phased return over up to four weeks is available at full pay.
