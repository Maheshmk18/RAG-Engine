# Code of Conduct

Owner: Legal. The Code of Conduct describes the standards of behaviour expected from everyone who works for Kestrel Systems.

## Respect at Work

Kestrel Systems does not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind. This applies in the office, at remote meetings, at company events and on company messaging channels. Concerns can be raised with a manager, People Operations or through the Speak Up line.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts and hospitality with a value of up to 50 from customers or suppliers. Anything worth more than 50 must be declared in the gifts register within five working days, and may need to be declined or returned. Cash or cash equivalents, such as gift cards, must never be accepted, regardless of value.

Employees involved in an active procurement process must not accept any gifts or hospitality from bidders.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation where their personal interests could conflict with the interests of Kestrel Systems. Examples include a close relative working for a supplier, or holding a significant investment in a competitor. Disclosures are made through the conflicts of interest form in the HR Portal.

## Outside Employment

Employees may take on outside work, including freelancing or a second job, only with written approval from their manager and People Operations. Outside work must not compete with Kestrel Systems or use company equipment, time or information.

## Social Media

Only authorised spokespeople may speak on behalf of Kestrel Systems. When posting about the company, employees should make clear that views are their own and must not share Confidential or Restricted information.

## Speak Up

The Speak Up line is an independent service for raising concerns about misconduct, fraud or safety. Reports can be made anonymously by phone or online, and are reviewed by the Legal team within seven days. Kestrel Systems does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Breaches

Breaches of the Code of Conduct may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
