# Sick Leave and Medical Absence

Owner: People Operations. This policy explains how to report sickness and what pay applies during medical absence.

## Reporting an Absence

If you are unable to work because of illness, notify your line manager by 10:00 on the first day of absence, by phone or direct message. Email alone is not sufficient notification. You must also record the absence in the HR Portal on the day you return.

If your manager is unavailable, contact People Operations at people@kestrel.example.

## Self-Certification and Medical Notes

For absences of up to seven calendar days, you can self-certify through the HR Portal. For absences longer than seven calendar days, a note from a doctor or other medical practitioner is required and must be uploaded to the HR Portal within three days of it being issued.

Frequent short-term absence, defined as four or more separate absences in a rolling twelve-month period, triggers a supportive conversation with your manager.

## Sick Pay

Employees receive full pay for up to 10 days of sickness absence in any rolling twelve-month period. After that, company sick pay continues at half pay for a further 20 days. Beyond this, statutory sick pay applies where the employee is eligible.

Sick pay is available from the first day of employment and is not affected by probation.

## Return to Work

After any absence of five working days or more, your manager will hold a short return-to-work meeting on your first day back. The meeting confirms that you are well enough to work and whether any adjustments are needed.

A phased return, where hours increase gradually over up to four weeks, can be agreed for employees returning from long-term illness. Phased returns are paid at full salary.

## Medical Appointments

Routine medical and dental appointments should be scheduled outside core hours where possible. Where this is not possible, up to four hours per appointment can be taken as paid time off with manager approval.

## Occupational Health

Employees absent for more than four consecutive weeks will be offered a referral to the occupational health service. The referral is confidential, and reports are only shared with the employee's consent.
