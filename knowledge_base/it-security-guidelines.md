# IT Security Guidelines

Owner: Security Team. These guidelines apply to everyone who uses Kestrel Systems devices, accounts or data.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must not be reused across the last ten passwords. Sharing passwords between employees is strictly prohibited.

Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account. Use the Kestrel Authenticator app; SMS codes are only allowed as a temporary fallback for up to seven days. Employees must store work passwords in the company password manager.

## Devices

Laptops must be locked with Windows key plus L, or Control plus Command plus Q on a Mac, whenever you leave your desk. Devices lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Full-disk encryption is enabled on every company laptop and must not be disabled.

Software must be installed through the Self Service portal. Installing unapproved software, including browser extensions that request access to all websites, is not allowed. USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops; use the approved file-sharing service to transfer files.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to security@kestrel.example within one hour of noticing it is missing. The Security Team will remotely lock and wipe the device. A delay in reporting a lost device is treated as a security incident.

## Remote Access

When working outside the office, you must connect through the company VPN before accessing internal systems or customer data. Do not use public Wi-Fi to access sensitive systems without the VPN.

## Email and Phishing

Be alert to phishing. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders, and check the sender's address carefully before acting on requests for payments or credentials. Report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing button in Outlook. The Security Team runs simulated phishing campaigns every quarter.

## Physical Security

A clean desk policy applies in all offices. Confidential papers must not be left unattended and must be placed in the secure shredding bins at the end of each day. Visitors must sign in at reception and be accompanied at all times.

## Security Training

All employees complete security awareness training within their first 30 days and then once every year. Failing to complete training within the deadline results in access to company systems being suspended until it is done.
