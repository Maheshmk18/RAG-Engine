# Travel and Expenses Policy

Owner: Finance. This policy explains what business costs Kestrel Systems reimburses and how to claim them.

## Submitting Claims

Expense claims are submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed. Itemised receipts are required for every expense over 25. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll run.

Claims up to 1,000 are approved by the employee's line manager. Claims above 1,000 also require approval from the department Director.

## Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in Expensify within 14 days of the statement date. Personal spending on a corporate card is not allowed, and any accidental personal charge must be reported and repaid within 30 days.

## Flights

Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or more. Business class requires approval from a member of the executive team. All flights must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days in advance where possible.

## Hotels

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 200 per night. In London, New York and San Francisco the limit is 275 per night. Hotels should be booked through the travel portal so that the company can locate employees in an emergency.

## Meals and Subsistence

While travelling, meal costs are reimbursed up to a daily limit of 60 for domestic travel and 90 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client entertainment events approved in advance by a Director.

## Ground Transport and Mileage

Public transport should be used where practical. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for journeys where public transport is unavailable, unsafe or would add more than one hour to the journey. Use of a personal car for business journeys is reimbursed at 0.45 per mile. Commuting between home and the usual office is not reimbursed.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed: fines and penalties, personal entertainment, upgrades that were not approved, travel insurance (employees are covered by the company policy), and costs for accompanying family members.
