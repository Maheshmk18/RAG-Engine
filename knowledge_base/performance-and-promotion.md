# Performance Reviews and Promotion

Owner: People Operations. This document describes how performance is reviewed and how promotions are decided.

## Review Cycle

Kestrel Systems runs two performance reviews each year. The mid-year review takes place in July and focuses on progress and development. The year-end review takes place in January and determines the performance rating.

Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from at least two peers chosen with the manager, and a conversation between the employee and their manager.

## Ratings

Year-end ratings use a five-point scale: 1 Not Meeting Expectations, 2 Partially Meeting Expectations, 3 Meeting Expectations, 4 Exceeding Expectations and 5 Exceptional. Ratings are calibrated across each department to make sure they are applied consistently.

Employees who joined after 1 October do not receive a year-end rating in their first review cycle.

## Salary Review

Salary increases are based on the year-end rating and market data. Increases take effect on 1 April each year and are confirmed to employees in writing in March.

## Promotions

Promotion decisions are made twice a year, in the January and July cycles. Managers nominate employees for promotion with a written case describing how the employee already performs at the next level. Promotions are reviewed by a panel of senior leaders from outside the employee's immediate team.

Employees must normally have been in their current role for at least 12 months before being considered for promotion.

## Performance Improvement Plans

If performance is below expectations, the manager will first discuss concerns informally. If performance does not improve, a formal performance improvement plan lasting 60 days is agreed, with clear goals and weekly check-ins. People Operations is involved in every formal plan.

## Appeals

Employees who disagree with their year-end rating can appeal in writing to People Operations within 14 days of receiving it. The appeal is reviewed by a manager who was not involved in the original rating.
